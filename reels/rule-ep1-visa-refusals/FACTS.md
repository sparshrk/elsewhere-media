# FACTS — rule-ep1-visa-refusals ("UK student visa refusals — the rule nobody tells you")

Every fact below is sourced directly from gov.uk, checked 24 Nov 2026 (UTC).

1. **The 28-day continuous-funds rule.**
   - https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/money — "You must have this money for at least 28 days in a row. The end
     date of the 28-day period must be within 31 days of the date you apply for your visa."
   - https://www.gov.uk/guidance/financial-evidence-for-student-and-child-student-route-applicants (last
     updated 31 Dec 2025) — "You must have had enough money for 28 days in a row... counting back from the date
     of the closing balance on the most recent evidence you provide." / evidence must be "from no more than 31
     days before the date you apply."
   - The film's claim ("miss the streak by one day and that evidence doesn't count") restates this continuous
     28-day requirement directly; it is not an independent/unverified claim. TRA-158 flagged a prior sourcing
     issue on a "28-day" claim — this version is checked directly against the two gov.uk pages above.

2. **Current (pre-30-Nov-2026) amounts.** https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/money, checked 24 Nov 2026 —
   £1,529/month (London), £1,171/month (outside London), up to 9 months.

3. **New amounts effective 30 November 2026.** Statement of Changes to the Immigration Rules, HC 584, 3
   September 2026 (gov.uk): https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/statement-of-changes-to-the-immigration-rules-hc-584-3-september-2026/statement-of-changes-to-the-immigration-rules-hc-584-3-september-2026-accessible
   — "In ST 12.3(a), for '£1529' substitute '£1570'." / "In ST 12.3(b), for '£1171' substitute '£1203'." / "In
   ST 12.4, for '£1529' substitute '£1570'." Effective 30 Nov 2026; applications before that date use the rules
   in force on 29 Nov 2026 (the £1,529/£1,171 figures). Checked 24 Nov 2026.

4. **Months covered.** Up to 9 months, Appendix Student ST12.3 (gov.uk Immigration Rules), re-checked 24 Nov 2026.

## What the film does NOT claim
Does not claim gov.uk explicitly uses the words "refused for one day" — the film's framing is a restatement of
the continuous-28-day definition, not a quotation of a refusal clause. No figures from consultancy/aggregator
blogs are used on screen; such sites were only used to locate which Statement of Changes to check, then every
number was verified against the gov.uk HC 584 page itself.
